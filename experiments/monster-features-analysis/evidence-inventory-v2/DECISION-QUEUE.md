# Prioritized decision queue — evidence only

No new dataset-wide defaults are approved by this queue. The five sampled
Multiattack recipes and three sampled fire strengths retain their *specific*
approvals. The primary agent must present a recommended smallest shared rule
and seek approval on material choices before building the second trial.

1. **Natural weapons and strength.** Review the 634/279/115 structured exact
   Bite/Claw/Tail occurrences by meaningful behavior, not name. Separate mode,
   base, added component and on-hit rule; compare fixed `1` Bite `(237, actions,
   0)`, two dragons `(39/106, actions, 1-3)`, grapple `(52, actions, 1)` and
   prone `(30, actions, 3)`. Decide what numeric differences can collapse and
   what independently useful strengths warrant separate parts; do not assume
   the red-dragon reference set or unlimited numeric tiers.
2. **Attack shapes and source conflicts.** Set treatment for fixed damage,
   zero-damage hit effects `(864, actions, 3)`, auto-hits `(1578, actions, 1)`,
   variable damage type `(684, actions, 1)`, and mixed-type rider `(1974,
   actions, 1)`. Decide how to handle melee/ranged source contradiction on
   Bugbear Javelin `(402, actions, 1)` without inventing an override or
   dropping a selected mode. Keep same-name save actions distinct (Xenk 2622).
3. **Compact monster bonuses and explicit included contributions.** Choose
   treatment for the 207 monsters with differing structured attack bonuses,
   257 with differing first damage bonuses, 33 with no usable attack bonus,
   and Brute/Enlarge/other already-included dice. Check mixed weapon/spell
   attacks and base-versus-conditional prose before freezing one profile or
   adopting a no-evidence fallback. Do not double-count extra dice.
4. **Recipe and prerequisite vocabulary.** Decide how a Multiattack can replace
   an attack with a casting use, starting with `(170, actions, 0)`; keep its
   specific attack references and timing. Decide how mythic activation and
   legendary instructions depend on their owning monster's other bound parts;
   absence must not silently grant an attack. The sampled five recipes are
   retained, not treated as proof of general substitution coverage.
5. **Spellcasting and other prose families.** Compare slot-based and at-will/day
   repertoires `(11/296/170, spellcasting, 0)`, component exceptions, footer
   restrictions and dependent features. Choose grouping that keeps useful
   differences without making every monster's spell list a default package or
   erasing it. Review Parry/Change Shape/Teleport and other same-name features
   by triggers, target eligibility and consequences, not name frequency.

After these decisions, a **bounded second trial** can select complete profiles
exposing contrasting families and validation failures documented in
`candidate-v1/NEXT-STEPS.md`. Nothing here authorizes full-dataset conversion,
production migration, generic overrides or changes to candidate-v1 outputs.
